# Lead Qualification Framework — Christine Laikind
### Brand Foundation + Content Engine + Get Found Audit
### ICP: Bookkeeping firm owners & solopreneur bookkeepers, $250K–$1M revenue

---

## 1. Qualifying Questions

Written as natural DM questions to weave into conversation (from Prompt 6/8 flows), not a survey to fire off all at once.

1. **Revenue range:** "Roughly what range is your firm doing right now — under $250K, $250K-$1M, or beyond that?"
2. **Team size:** "Is it just you right now, or do you have a team?"
3. **Current bottleneck:** "What's the actual bottleneck right now — not enough new clients, not enough time to find them, or something else?"
4. **Prior attempts:** "Have you tried fixing this yourself already, or would this be the first real attempt at it?"
5. **Decision authority:** "Would this be your call to make, or is there someone else who'd need to weigh in?"
6. **Timeline/urgency:** "Is this a 'need to fix soon' thing, or more of a 'thinking ahead' thing right now?"

---

## 2. Scoring Rubric

| Tier | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Qualified** | Revenue in $250K-$1M range, has at least one real buying trigger present (referrals slowed, hit a ceiling, etc.), sole or clear decision authority, timeline is "soon" not "someday" |
| **Maybe** | In range but urgency is low ("thinking ahead"), OR needs partner/spouse sign-off, OR hasn't tried anything yet and is still early in recognizing the problem |
| **Not a fit** | Revenue well outside $250K-$1M, no real bottleneck they can name, pure curiosity with no intent, or clearly price-shopping with no context |

---

## 3. Disqualifying Signals

1. **Revenue far outside range** — under $100K (not enough at stake yet to justify investment) or well over $1M (likely needs a different scale of solution/team, not a solo-consultant engagement)
2. **No real bottleneck articulated** — if someone can't name what's actually broken beyond "I guess I should post more," they're not far enough into the problem to convert yet
3. **Price-shopping without context** — asking for the price immediately with zero interest in the actual problem or process is a weak signal, not a strong one, for a $950-$4,500 investment
4. **No authority and no path to authority** — "I'd have to ask my business partner" is fine if there's a real next step; "I'd have to ask my business partner, who's not really involved in this stuff" is a stall
5. **Pure information-gathering with no timeline** — someone doing competitive research or "just curious what's out there" with no stated urgency or trigger event

---

## 4. The Filter Message

A natural 2-3 message sequence to run in DM before ever mentioning the calendar link — this should feel like continuing the conversation, not switching into a screening process.

**Message 1 (after initial rapport is established):**
> Can I ask — roughly what range is your firm doing right now? Just want to make sure what I'm about to suggest actually fits your situation.

**Message 2 (after they answer):**
> Got it, that's helpful. And is it mostly just you right now, or do you have a team helping with delivery?

**Message 3 (transition to next step):**
> Makes sense. Based on what you've told me, [reference their specific bottleneck] — that's actually exactly what I built the Get Found Audit for. Want me to walk you through what that looks like, or do you have questions first?

If the answers land in "Qualified" territory, this naturally leads into offering the calendar link. If "Maybe," move to the nurture path below instead of pushing toward a call.

---

## 5. What To Do With "Maybe"

**Nurture path for close-but-not-ready leads:**

1. **Send them the Visibility Ledger self-assessment** if they haven't done it yet — a low-friction way to build more self-awareness of the problem without you having to push
2. **Add them to a loose follow-up cadence** — not aggressive outreach, but a check-in every 2-3 weeks referencing something relevant (a post, an insight, "thought of you when I saw this")
3. **Point them to specific content, not everything** — send the 1-2 most relevant posts from your content calendar that speak directly to their stated bottleneck, rather than a generic "check out my page"
4. **Re-ask the qualifying questions in 4-6 weeks**, framed casually ("Hey, curious how things have been going since we talked") — urgency and revenue can both shift, and today's "maybe" is often next month's "qualified"
5. **Don't remove them from your radar** — a "maybe" that never gets nurtured just quietly disappears; a "maybe" that gets one thoughtful touch every few weeks often converts later without ever feeling chased
