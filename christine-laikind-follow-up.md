# Follow-Up Sequence — Christine Laikind
### Brand Foundation + Content Engine + Get Found Audit
### ICP: Bookkeeping firm owners & solopreneur bookkeepers, $250K–$1M revenue

---

## Context

For prospects who engaged (replied to outreach, completed the Visibility Ledger, or had a first conversation) but didn't book a call or went quiet after.

---

## The 5-Touch Sequence

### Touch 1 — Day 1-2
**Purpose:** Reconnect naturally, no pressure — re-open the thread with something specific, not a generic nudge.

> Hey [Name] — following up on where we left off. Still thinking through [specific thing they mentioned — the referral slowdown, the profile concern, etc.]? No rush, just wanted to check in.

---

### Touch 2 — Day 4-5
**Purpose:** Value-add — give them something useful with zero ask, to rebuild momentum without pressure.

> Hey [Name] — saw this and thought of our conversation: [share a specific, relevant insight, post, or observation tied to what they told you]. Thought it might be useful even outside of anything we discussed.

---

### Touch 3 — Day 7-8
**Purpose:** Social proof / normalizing — show them they're not alone in this, without a hard pitch.

> Hey [Name] — something I hear constantly from bookkeeping firm owners in your range: [specific pattern relevant to their situation, e.g. "the referral slowdown feels sudden but it's usually been building for a while"]. Curious if that's matched what you've been noticing.

---

### Touch 4 — Day 10-11
**Purpose:** Direct ask — after three lower-pressure touches, make a clear, specific offer to move forward.

> Hey [Name] — want to just get something on the calendar to walk through this properly? Here's my link if it's easier than back-and-forth: [CALENDAR_LINK]. No pressure if the timing's not right, just want to make it easy if it is.

---

### Touch 5 — Day 14 (the "should I close this out" message)
**Purpose:** Permission to close, without sounding annoyed or final — leaves the door genuinely open.

> Hey [Name] — I don't want to keep filling your inbox if the timing just isn't right, so I'll leave this as my last check-in for now. If things change or the referral situation shifts, I'm here — just reach out anytime. Either way, good luck with the firm.

---

## Signals to Stop the Sequence Early

1. **A clear, direct "not interested" or "not right now" response** — respect it immediately rather than continuing the cadence; a forced "just checking in" after an explicit no reads as pushy and damages the relationship for any future re-engagement.

2. **They mention a real, specific blocker outside your control** (e.g. "we're mid-acquisition," "I'm dealing with a health issue," "just had a baby") — pause the sequence entirely and follow up organically much later rather than on the standard cadence; this isn't a sales timing issue, it's a life circumstance.

---

## Notes

- Each touch has a different job on purpose — reconnect, add value, normalize, ask directly, then release. Repeating "just checking in" across multiple touches is the most common way follow-up sequences fail.
- Keep every message DM-length, not email-length — this is a continuation of a conversation, not a new pitch each time.
- Touch 4 is the only touch in the sequence that includes the calendar link — introducing it earlier undercuts the lower-pressure tone of touches 1-3.
