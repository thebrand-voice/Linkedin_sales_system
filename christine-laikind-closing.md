## 1. DM-to-Call Bridge

Three ways to move a warm DM conversation into a booked call, each tied to something specific the prospect actually said — never a cold "let's hop on a call."

**1. Tied to their stated bottleneck:**
> Based on everything you've told me about [specific bottleneck they named], I think it's worth actually walking through this properly rather than doing it over DM. Want to grab 20 minutes?

**2. Tied to the Visibility Ledger result:**
> Your result landed in [band name] — that's really common, and there's a specific reason it usually shows up that way. Easier to explain on a quick call than typing it all out. You open to that?

**3. Tied to momentum in the conversation:**
> This has been a genuinely useful conversation — feels like the natural next step is getting something on the calendar so I can actually show you what this would look like for your firm specifically. Want me to send my link?

## 2. Call Structure

Not a rigid script — a flow to keep the conversation anchored while staying natural.

**1. Opening (2-3 min)**
Light, human, not rushed into business. Reference something specific from prior conversation to show continuity.

**2. Discovery (8-10 min)**
Ask open questions about their firm, their current client-acquisition situation, and what's actually changed recently. Let them do most of the talking here — resist the urge to start pitching early.

*Bookkeeping-specific note:* This is where cash-flow awareness and ROI-mindedness tend to surface naturally — they may start doing mental math out loud about what a new client is worth. Let that happen; it does your value-framing work for you. Don't rush past it to get to your pitch.

**3. Positioning (5-7 min)**
Reflect back what they told you, then position the system as the direct answer to *their specific situation* — not a generic feature walkthrough. Name the exact bottleneck they described and connect it to the specific piece of the system (Audit, Brand Foundation, or Content Engine) that addresses it.

**4. Price conversation (3-5 min)**
State the price directly and early in this section — no hedging. Offer the entry point (Audit) and the full bundle, and let them ask about the difference rather than over-explaining unprompted.

*Bookkeeping-specific note:* Skepticism toward "coaches" and marketing generally is common here — if it surfaces, address it directly rather than talking around it (see Objection Handling matrix, #5).

**5. The ask (2-3 min)**
Ask directly for the decision. Don't let the call end vaguely.

## 3. Ways to Ask for the Close

**1. Direct:**
> Based on everything we've talked through, does it make sense to move forward with the Audit, or would you rather start with the full system?

**2. Confirming readiness:**
> Sounds like this is a real fit for where you're at. Ready to get started, or is there anything still unclear before you decide?

**3. Offering the natural next step:**
> I think the Audit is the right starting point given what you've described. Want me to send over what's needed to get that scheduled?

All three are direct without being pushy — appropriate for a numbers-driven buyer who generally responds better to clarity than to soft-pedaling.

---

## 4. If They Say "Let Me Think About It" On the Call

Don't pressure — get specific about what "thinking about it" actually covers, and give them a clear next step regardless of their answer.

**Response:**
> Totally fair. Can I ask what specifically you want to think through — is it more the investment, the timing, or something about whether this is the right fit? Just want to make sure I've actually answered the real question, not just talked at you.

**Then, regardless of their answer:**
> No problem either way. I'll send a quick summary of what we covered so you have it in front of you. Want me to check back in [specific timeframe, e.g. "next week"], or would you rather reach out when you're ready?

This keeps the door open without leaving it vague — a specific follow-up point (not "let me know!") is what actually gets these conversations picked back up, and feeds directly into the follow-up sequence from Prompt 11 if they go quiet.
