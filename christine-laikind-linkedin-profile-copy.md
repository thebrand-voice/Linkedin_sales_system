## 1. Headline Options (under 220 characters)

**A. Outcome-led**
> I help bookkeeping firm owners ($250K–$1M) get found by the clients already searching for them — without becoming a "content creator." Brand + Content systems built once, working every week.

**B. Curiosity-led**
> Your referrals used to be enough. They're not anymore — and it's not a marketing problem, it's a visibility problem. I fix that for bookkeeping firms doing $250K–$1M.

**C. Credibility-led**
> Brand + Content systems for bookkeeping firm owners ready to stop relying on referrals. Get Found Audit → Brand Foundation → Content Engine. Built for bookkeepers, not generic small businesses.

**Trade-off notes:**
- **A** converts fastest with people who already know they have a visibility problem — leads with the promise.
- **B** is strongest for cold profile visitors who haven't fully named their problem yet — the "referrals used to be enough" line does the diagnosing for them.
- **C** is best if you're already getting decent inbound curiosity and just need the profile to confirm credibility and relevance fast — less persuasive, more confirming.

**Recommendation:** Start with B. It does the most work turning a passive scroller into someone who reads the About section, because it names the exact shift they're feeling before they've articulated it themselves.
