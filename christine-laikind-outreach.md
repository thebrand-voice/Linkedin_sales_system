# Outreach Messages — Christine Laikind
### Brand Foundation + Content Engine + Get Found Audit
### ICP: Bookkeeping firm owners & solopreneur bookkeepers, $250K–$1M revenue

---

## Context

These are first-touch DMs for people who accepted a connection request but haven't engaged with content or commented on the mini-audit yet — so there's no prior conversation to reference, just their profile/business context.

---

## 3 Variations

### 1. Trigger-based
*(references a likely recent event — losing a client, hitting capacity, scaling struggles)*

> Hey [Name] — thanks for connecting. [PERSONALIZATION: reference something specific from their profile/business, e.g. "Noticed you've been running [Firm Name] for a few years now"] — curious, has referral flow stayed steady for you this year, or has it gotten quieter like it has for a lot of firms I talk to?

**Follow-up if they reply with interest but no commitment:**
> That tracks with what I'm hearing a lot right now. It's usually not a work-quality thing — it's that nothing's actively bringing new people in when referrals slow down. I put together a quick self-assessment that shows exactly where that gap tends to be for firms like yours, if you want me to send it over.

---

### 2. Insight-based
*(leads with a useful observation about their business/industry stage)*

> Hey [Name] — thanks for connecting. [PERSONALIZATION: specific observation, e.g. "Your focus on [niche] clients is clear from your profile, which honestly a lot of bookkeeping firms could learn from"] — one thing I notice a lot with firms that niche well like that: the positioning is strong but the visibility side hasn't caught up yet. Has that been true for you at all, or is your pipeline in a good spot?

**Follow-up if they reply with interest but no commitment:**
> Makes sense. That gap between "clear on who we serve" and "actually visible to them" is exactly what I work on with bookkeeping firms. I've got a short self-assessment that pinpoints where it's showing up specifically — want it?

---

### 3. Curiosity-based
*(references a result/pattern without explaining it yet)*

> Hey [Name] — thanks for connecting. Random question: have you noticed most of your new clients over the last year came from the same one or two sources, or has it been pretty mixed?

**Follow-up if they reply with interest but no commitment:**
> That's the pattern I see constantly with bookkeeping firms in your revenue range — one or two channels doing all the work, which feels fine until one of them slows down. I built a quick self-assessment that shows where the biggest gap actually is for a given firm. Want me to send it your way?

---

## Notes

- **No pitch, no offer, no link in the first message** — all three variations end in a genuine, specific question designed to earn a real reply, not a "yes/no" to an offer
- **[PERSONALIZATION] placeholder** in variations 1 and 2 needs a real, specific detail pulled from their profile each time — years in business, niche, recent post, team size, anything that proves you looked at them individually
- **Variation 3 works well at higher volume** since it doesn't require deep profile research — good option once you're sending outreach beyond just your best-fit prospects
- Every follow-up routes to the mini-audit (once built), not straight to the paid Audit — this keeps the ask small and matches the funnel logic from Prompts 5 and 6
