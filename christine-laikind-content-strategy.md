# 30-Day Content Strategy — Christine Laikind
### Brand Foundation + Content Engine + Get Found Audit
### ICP: Bookkeeping firm owners & solopreneur bookkeepers, $250K–$1M revenue

---

## Core Positioning (everything below is built on this)

*"Former marketing executive + 3x business owner, applying marketing expertise to a profession that's never had it built for them."*

Segments to write for:
- **Capped Solo** — $250K-$450K, time-starved, hesitant about marketing, needs the low-friction Get Found Audit ($950) entry point
- **Stuck Firm Owner** — $450K-$1M, has team/capacity, needs full Brand Foundation + Content Engine, ready for the $4,500 bundle if trust is established

---

## The 4 Content Pillars

1. **"The Gap"** — the specific pattern of brilliant-with-numbers-but-invisible-online that you noticed as a marketing exec. This is your signature observation — own it.
2. **"Marketing Translated"** — marketing concepts most bookkeepers were never taught, explained in their language, with zero jargon.
3. **"What's Actually Broken"** — diagnostic, myth-busting content that shows your thinking without giving away the full system (natural Audit-lead-in territory).
4. **"Behind the System"** — transparent build-in-public content about Brand Foundation / Content Engine / Get Found Audit, since you don't have client case studies yet — the system itself and your own process becomes the proof.

## Repurposing candidates (strongest 4 for turning into your lead magnet)

- **Day 9** ("The #1 thing on a bookkeeper's LinkedIn profile that quietly costs them clients") — this is close to a diagnostic checklist already; good raw material for the audit-adjacent lead magnet
- **Day 17** (most common profile mistake) — same territory, different angle; combine with Day 9 for depth
- **Day 22** ("3 things I check first in a Get Found Audit") — literally previews your paid offer at low cost, strong lead magnet foundation
- **Day 6 / Day 18** (referral pipeline going quiet) — strongest emotional hook of the batch; good as the lead magnet's opening frame

---

## Notes on the funnel math

- TOFU is intentionally the heaviest lane early in the month to build reach before asking for anything — you have no case studies yet, so trust has to be earned through demonstrated thinking (pillars 1 and 3) before any BOFU post will convert
- Every BOFU post routes to the **Audit ($950)**, not the bundle — that's your lowest-friction ask until you have 2-3 real client outcomes to point to
- Once you complete your first 1-2 Audits, swap 2-3 of the "Behind the System" posts for real before/after content — that's the fastest way to unlock stronger BOFU posts and, eventually, the bundle pitch
